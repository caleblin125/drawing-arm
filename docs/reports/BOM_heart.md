## Hardware

- 1 x N20-style 6 V gearmotor (D-shaft 3 mm), the ONLY motor
- 1 x 8 mm steel rod with D-flat, shoulder axle; 1 x 8 mm steel rod with D-flat, camshaft
- ~12 x 4 mm steel pins / M4 shoulder bolts (link joints, follower pivots), 1 x 4 mm latch pin
- 4 x 684ZZ bearings (4x9x4) as rollers + 4 mm stub pins
- 2 x 3 mm dowels (bell-crank key, M3 hub key)
- 2 x M3 x ~90 mm screws (carriage posts), M3 screws for the stop posts
- Bowden cable (inner ~0.8 mm, housing ~2 mm OD) + 1 compression spring for the pen carrier
- washers 8 mm / 4 mm (1 mm thick) between layers

## Torsion springs (shoulder axle)

- M1: music wire 1.4 mm, mean D 17.9 mm, 6 coils, wind 149 deg, direction CCW on the hub
- M2: music wire 1.2 mm, mean D 19.7 mm, 7 coils, wind 259 deg, direction CCW on the hub
- M3: music wire 1.1 mm, mean D 19.6 mm, 13 coils, wind 247 deg, direction CW on the hub

## Notes

- pen carrier: compression spring (OD < 24 mm, ~1 N at pen-down) between the hand collar top and the carrier flange; cable from the pen lever eye through the Bowden stop on the hand; pen lift 3 mm
- motor: N20-style gearmotor ~210 rpm (200 rpm part + voltage trim), single-start worm m1.5 -> 30:1 wheel -> camshaft 7.0 rpm (8.6 s per drawing); worm is self-locking
- latch: a 4 mm pin through the base ear and the carriage tongue (horizontal hole, drill/ream)
